# App icon: 02a Lit body, final

**Decision (2026-11-08):** the user said "let's go with lit body (2a), but when the logo shows in the app, it's a list
mark."

- **The app icon** is the 02a lit body: the whole body is a lit Ember tile with a white check, in light and dark alike.
- **Inside the app**, the mark is the 02b list mark: the 20×16 lit tile every list wears, in Ember, with the check.
  `TileLayer` and `TileArt` draw it in code; it isn't a picture. The Mac's sidebar header and About panel show it
  (`app/Tasks/Sidebar/SidebarHeader.swift`, `ListMark`), and nothing here feeds them.

The canvas is https://claude.ai/artifact/3YwirhrEHxooT5pJcRoHN1. Round 3 is on the "Round 3 · lit" page, with notes in
`../round2/README.md`. It's private until shared from its Share menu. The 04 Log icon this replaces (2026-10-31) is in
git history and on round 1's canvas.

Everything here is generated by `export.sh` from the geometry in `gen.py`. Edit `gen.py`, then rerun:

    design/logo/final/export.sh

It needs python3, with no packages, and Xcode 26 or later for swiftc, iconutil and Icon Composer's ictool.

## Files

| Path | What |
|---|---|
| `gen.py` | The geometry, the colours and the finish. |
| `svg/icon.svg` | The macOS 1024 master on the Big Sur grid: an 824 body at 100, with continuous corners. |
| `svg/icon-small.svg` | The cut used below 60px: a heavier check, and no ring or strip, since both would fall under a pixel. |
| `svg/ios.svg`, `svg/ios-tinted.svg` | The iOS 1024 masters. They're full bleed; the system masks the corners. |
| `png/icon_*.png` | Every macOS size (16, 32, 128, 256 and 512, each @1x and @2x) in iconset naming. Pixel sizes below 60 (16, 16@2x and 32) use the small cut. |
| `AppIcon.icns` | The same PNGs as a classic icon file. |
| `AppIcon.icon/` | The Icon Composer document the Mac app ships, copied to `app/Tasks/AppIcon.icon`. It has an Ember fill and two layers, `finish.svg` (the light and the strip) and `check.svg`. |
| `system/*.png` | What macOS 26 and later draws from `AppIcon.icon` in each appearance: Default, Dark, Tinted and Clear, each light and dark. ictool renders them. |
| `render.swift` | Converts SVG to PNG through AppKit, so no extra tools are needed. |

`export.sh` also writes the iPhone's icon set, `ios/Tasks/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset`, as three 1024s:

- `icon-1024.png` for any appearance;
- `icon-1024-dark.png`, the same lit body (the user asked for it in light and dark);
- `icon-1024-tinted.png`, a greyscale cut (white check on black) that the system tints.

`ios/Tools/MakeIcon.swift`, which drew the old 04 Log icon, is gone. Inside the iPhone app the mark is the list mark too
(`LogoMark` in `ios/Tasks/Views/Components.swift`).

## The finish

The finish is the list marks', from `app/TasksCore/Sources/TasksTheme/TileArt.swift`. It's drawn at the reference
tile's scale (`docs/feedback/reference-tile.svg`): one unit is 1/45.54 of the body's height, 18.1 on the macOS body.
`../round2/README.md` has the details. Bottom to top:

- **Halo:** 0.5 unit outside the edge, in black. One PNG serves both appearances, so it takes the light appearance's
  `tileHalo`, 18%. At 35% (dark's value) it read as a grey outline on light Finder windows. The `.icon` has no halo,
  because macOS draws the shape's edge and shadow itself.
- **Fill:** #E6683D, the accent.
- **Light:** radial from the top middle, with radii of 1.4 × width and 1.1 × height, using `TileArt.light(d)` at its 15
  stops.
- **Specular:** a 1-unit strip along the top: `TileArt.specularHalf`, mirrored, ×1.44 (the reference's 1-unit
  amounts).
- **Ring:** 1 unit inside the edge, in white at 10%. It's in the PNGs only; in the `.icon` the system draws the edge.
- **Check:** white and flat, like a tile's glyph. It's the canvas's `ember-lit` check, stroke 100, or 120 in the small
  cut, drawn as two capsules so that every renderer agrees.

## Appearances (macOS 26 and later, `AppIcon.icon`)

- **Default and Dark** are the same lit body. Dark is given explicitly in `icon.json`; without it, the system makes its
  own dark version, with a dark body and an Ember check.
- **Tinted** is the system's own: it tints the body and keeps the check white.
- **Clear** is the system's own as well. Icon Composer has no clear key.
- **Glass and translucency** are off, and the shadow is neutral at 30%, as before.
