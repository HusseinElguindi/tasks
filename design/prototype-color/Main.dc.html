Tasks
⌘N (⌥N here)New task, opened in the details with the cursor in its title. Return goes on to the notes; Esc is done.
↩ · double-clickEdit the title: in the details, or in the row, as the strip's Edit tasks says. Esc cancels.
Space · ⌘KComplete or uncomplete. Space on a heading: collapse or expand
⌥⌘N (⌃⌥N here)New heading, above the selected task (or at the bottom). Return names it; Tab goes on to its notes.
⌘D · ›Show or hide details
⌘S · ⇧⌘DWhen… · Deadline… (type tom, fri, 3d)
⌘T (⌥T here)When: Today
⇧⌘MMove to… (⌘1–9 picks)
⌥⌘↑ ⌥⌘↓Move up or down. A heading moves with its tasks. In Upcoming: a day earlier or later
⌘⌫Delete (Undo in the notice, or ⌘Z). In Trash: Put Back
Notes
⌘B · ⌘IBold, italic: the selection, or the word at the caret. Again to remove.
↩ · ⇧↩Next list item (Return on an empty one ends the list) · a plain line break
Tab · ⇧TabIndent or outdent a list item
⌥⇧⌘VPaste as plain text (rich text pastes as Markdown; a link pasted over text links it)
Esc · ⌘↩Done; the notes keep what's typed
Moving around
↑ ↓ · ⇧ · ⌘-clickSelect, extend, add to the selection
← → · TabSidebar, list, details
⌘1–9 (⌃1–9 here)The sidebar's first nine rows. Hold ⌘ to see them.
Type lettersJump to a task or list by name
⇧⌘N (⌥⇧N here)New list
⌘Z · ⇧⌘ZUndo, redo
The browser keeps ⌘N, ⌘T and ⌘1–9 for itself, so this page also takes ⌥ or ⌃ for them. The Mac app uses the ⌘ keys.
