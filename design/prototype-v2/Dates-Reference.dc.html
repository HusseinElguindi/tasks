How dates read
Each row has the When and the Deadline on the same day, to show both. A deadline counts the whole days left, not its own day, up to 14; farther, the date. Overdue counts days at any distance. The When says “in 2d”, never “left”. A past When reads Today (it carries forward). Today is Sat, Oct 31.
